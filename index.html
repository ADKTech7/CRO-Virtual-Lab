Virtual Lab - Advanced CRO Measurement

Amplitude | Frequency | Phase Difference | Lissajous Analysis

Aim

To operate a dual-channel CRO and measure amplitude, frequency and phase difference of sinusoidal signals.

Experiment Instructions

  1. Connect the CH1 and CH2 probes to the signal source.
  2. Turn the required channel ON.
  3. Set VOLTS/DIV and TIME/DIV.
  4. Select AC, DC or GND coupling as required.
  5. Set signal amplitude, frequency and phase.
  6. Press RUN and observe the traces.
  7. Use cursor readings for time and voltage measurements.
  8. Switch to X-Y/Lissajous mode and compare the phase result.

Learning Outcomes

  • Explain vertical and horizontal CRO controls.
  • Measure Vp and Vpp.
  • Calculate f from T.
  • Determine phase from Δt and Lissajous patterns.
f = 1/T   |   Vpp = 2Vp   |   φ = (Δt/T) × 360°
