Welcome to the Virtual Laboratory

This experiment simulates a dual-channel CRO. Operate the virtual instruments, observe waveform divisions, perform calculations, record observations and complete the assessment.

Student task: Perform the experiment instead of simply reading the reference values.

Aim

To measure amplitude, frequency and phase difference between two sinusoidal signals using a dual-channel CRO and Lissajous pattern.

Learning Objectives

  • Set VOLTS/DIV and TIME/DIV.
  • Read waveform divisions.
  • Calculate Vp, Vpp, period and frequency.
  • Measure phase using time difference.
  • Interpret Lissajous patterns.

Theory

Vpp = vertical divisions × VOLTS/DIV

Vp = Vpp / 2

T = horizontal divisions × TIME/DIV

f = 1/T

Phase φ = (Δt/T) × 360°

In X-Y mode, two equal-frequency sinusoidal signals produce a Lissajous figure. Its shape depends on phase and amplitude ratio.

Prerequisites

  • AC waveform basics
  • Frequency and time period
  • Basic CRO controls
  • Phase difference

Apparatus

  • Dual-channel CRO
  • Function generator
  • CH1/CH2 probes
  • Connecting leads

Procedure

  1. Open the Simulation and switch the CRO to RUN.
  2. Connect CH1 to the generator.
  3. Select DC coupling and suitable VOLTS/DIV.
  4. Adjust TIME/DIV to display complete cycles.
  5. Determine Vpp and frequency from divisions.
  6. Connect CH2 and set the required phase.
  7. Use the two waveforms/time relationship to determine phase.
  8. Switch to X-Y/Lissajous mode and verify phase.
  9. Enter measured values in Observation and submit.
  10. Complete Assessment.
