Virtual Lab – CRO Measurement of Amplitude, Frequency and Phase

Electronics Engineering • Interactive Oscilloscope Experiment

Aim

To measure the amplitude, frequency and phase difference of sinusoidal signals using a virtual Cathode Ray Oscilloscope (CRO).

Theory

Amplitude: The peak value of a sinusoidal voltage is measured from the zero reference to the maximum vertical deflection.

Frequency: Frequency is calculated from the time period: f = 1/T.

Phase difference: For two sinusoidal signals, phase difference is related to the horizontal time displacement: φ = (Δt/T) × 360°.

Apparatus

  • Virtual CRO / Oscilloscope
  • Two-channel sinusoidal signal source
  • Connecting leads
  • Time/div and Volt/div controls

Procedure

  1. Set the amplitude and frequency of Channel 1.
  2. Set the amplitude, frequency and phase shift of Channel 2.
  3. Start the virtual CRO.
  4. Observe the waveforms on the oscilloscope screen.
  5. Use the displayed measurements to determine amplitude and frequency.
  6. For phase measurement, observe the time displacement between corresponding points of the two signals.
  7. Record the readings in the observation table.
